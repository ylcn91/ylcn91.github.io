    Questions

    What is Alp?

    An AI team that works in your company chat. You ask in Teams, Slack or Buzz; Alp answers from your data, opens and tracks the work in Jira, and puts specialist agents on it.

    Do we need engineers to use it?

    No. Marketing, support, sales and operations teams ask in plain language. Engineering work goes through the same chat, with plans and reviews built in.

    Which chat apps does it work in?

    Microsoft Teams, Slack and Buzz, and comments on Jira tasks. It replies in the same thread it was asked in.

    Who can see what?

    Alp works with the permissions of the person who asked, reads only approved chats, and never posts personal data to a chat; it gives counts and summaries instead.

    What happens when Alp gets something wrong?

    Work is reviewed and tested before anyone sees it, people approve plans and going live, and Alp rechecks its own numbers against the source every morning. You can pause every agent at once.

    How is it priced?

    On usage, on Alp Cloud. Talk to us and we will size it with you.
